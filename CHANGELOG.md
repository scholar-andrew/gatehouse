## 1.16.0 (2026-11-16)

- The launcher can be driven with a controller: the Steam Deck's own controls, a Steam Controller, or any pad Steam presents as a gamepad. - The arrow keys on a keyboard move the selection too, when a button or a row of tabs is selected.
