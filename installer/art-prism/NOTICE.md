# Prism Launcher shortcut art

These images are for the **Prism Launcher** Steam shortcut. They're made by `tools/make_prism_art.ps1` from Prism
Launcher's own logo, which is in `source/prism-logo.png`. That file was rendered at 1024×1024 from the scalable logo
that ships with Prism Launcher (`org.prismlauncher.PrismLauncher.svg`).

The logo is © Prism Launcher Contributors (<https://prismlauncher.org>). Prism Launcher is licensed under the GNU
General Public License v3.0, according to the licence files that ship with it. They give the logo no separate terms.
These images contain the logo, so they're shared under the same licence. They aren't made or endorsed by the Prism
Launcher project.

The October Journey art in `installer/art/` is original and separate.
